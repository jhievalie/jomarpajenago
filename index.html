E-commerce Sales & Marketing Dashboard

Power BI · DAX · SQL · Excel · Data Modeling

Problem

Sales and marketing data were spread across multiple sources (Excel, SQL databases, and platform exports), making it difficult to track performance consistently. Reporting required manual consolidation, which was time-consuming and prone to inconsistencies. There was no unified view of KPIs such as revenue, conversion rate, and channel performance, limiting the ability to make timely, data-driven decisions.

Process

I designed and developed an end-to-end BI solution using Power BI to centralize and automate reporting:

  • Consolidated raw data from multiple sources using SQL and Excel
  • Cleaned and transformed datasets to ensure consistency and accuracy
  • Built a structured data model optimized for performance and scalability
  • Created DAX measures for key KPIs (Revenue, AOV, CVR, ROAS)
  • Designed an interactive dashboard with dynamic filters (MTD, YTD, All Time)
  • Implemented funnel and channel performance analysis for deeper insights

The dashboard was built with a focus on clarity, usability, and decision-making speed.

Result

  • Reduced reporting time by up to 40% through automation
  • Enabled real-time monitoring of sales and marketing performance
  • Improved visibility into customer behavior and conversion funnel
  • Allowed faster identification of high-performing channels and campaigns

This solution transformed fragmented data into a centralized, decision-ready dashboard.

Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

Power BI · DAX · Power Query · Data Modeling

Project Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides business users with a comprehensive view of retail sales performance across different regions, customer segments, product categories, and time periods.

It was designed to help decision-makers monitor sales performance, identify trends, evaluate regional performance, and discover high-performing products and customers through interactive visualizations.

Business Objectives

  • Monitor overall sales performance
  • Identify top-performing products and customers
  • Compare sales across regions and categories
  • Analyze sales trends over time
  • Support data-driven business decisions

Business Questions

  • How much revenue was generated?
  • How many orders were placed?
  • How are sales trending over time?
  • Which regions perform best?
  • Which products generate the highest sales?
  • Who are the top customers?

Key Features

Executive KPIs

  • Total Sales
  • Total Orders
  • Average Order Value
  • Average Sales per Day

Interactive Visualizations

  • Monthly Sales Trend
  • Sales by Category
  • Sales by Region
  • Top 10 Products
  • Top 10 Customers

Interactive Filters

  • Year
  • Region
  • Category
  • Segment

Data & Process

The report follows a simple star-schema approach consisting of an Orders fact table, a Calendar date dimension, and a dedicated measures table for DAX calculations.

  • Validated data types
  • Converted and prepared date fields
  • Created a Calendar table
  • Performed data quality checks
  • Created model relationships
  • Built custom DAX measures

Result & Impact

The dashboard provides a centralized and interactive view of retail sales performance, making it easier to identify trends, compare regions and categories, and discover high-performing products and customers.
