Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

Power BI · DAX · Power Query · Data Modeling

Project Overview

An interactive Sales Performance Dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI to analyze retail sales performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance across different regions, customer segments, product categories, and time periods.

The project demonstrates data modeling, Power Query transformations, DAX calculations, and dashboard design following Power BI best practices.

Business Objectives

  • Monitor overall sales performance
  • Identify top-performing products and customers
  • Compare sales across regions and categories
  • Analyze sales trends over time
  • Support data-driven business decisions

Business Questions

  • How much revenue was generated?
  • How many orders were placed?
  • How are sales trending over time?
  • Which regions perform best?
  • Which products generate the highest sales?
  • Who are the top customers?

Key Features

Executive KPIs

  • Total Sales
  • Total Orders
  • Average Order Value
  • Average Sales per Day

Interactive Visualizations

  • Monthly Sales Trend
  • Sales by Category
  • Sales by Region
  • Top 10 Products
  • Top 10 Customers

Interactive Filters

  • Year
  • Region
  • Category
  • Segment

Data Model

The report follows a simple star-schema approach designed to support efficient reporting and analysis.

  • Orders — Fact Table
  • Calendar — Date Dimension
  • _Measures — DAX Measures

The model was structured to support time-based analysis and consistent business calculations across the dashboard.

Data & Process

Data preparation and reporting were handled using Power Query, DAX, and Power BI's data modeling capabilities.

  • Validated data types across the dataset
  • Converted and standardized date fields
  • Created a dedicated Calendar table
  • Performed data quality checks
  • Created model relationships
  • Built custom DAX measures for business KPIs

Key Insights

The dashboard enables users to explore retail performance and identify trends through interactive visualizations.

  • Monitor sales performance across different regions
  • Identify high-performing product categories
  • Discover top-performing products and customers
  • Analyze monthly sales trends
  • Compare performance across customer segments

Result & Impact

The dashboard provides business users with a centralized, interactive view of retail sales performance, making it easier to monitor KPIs, identify trends, compare performance, and support data-driven decisions.

Future Improvements

  • Drill-through pages
  • Bookmarks and navigation
  • Tooltip pages
  • Mobile-optimized layout
  • Custom Power BI visual (.pbiviz)
  • Additional business KPIs
